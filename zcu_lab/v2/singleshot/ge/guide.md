**Readers:** operator, agent. Calibrate readout from two IQ clouds acquired with the π probe off and on.

# Single-shot GE readout calibration

## What is measured

Each shot runs **Reset** (optional) → **Init Pulse** (optional) → **Probe Pulse** (off or on) → **Readout** → **Relax delay**. Run takes **Shots** readouts per branch, in one round per branch.

Primary rotates the IQ plane and fits the projected histograms to estimate the g/e centers, common width and preparation mixtures. The rotation angle and threshold define the projected boundary. Fidelity is the equal-weight assignment score for the two *prepared labels* at that boundary, not pure-state readout fidelity. Probe off/on can each contain a mixture, and a threshold can exploit differences within a single cluster.

## Key settings

- Before Run, verify **Probe Pulse** is a calibrated g↔e π pulse and **Readout** has calibrated settings and timing. Bind the intended readout module. If starting from the fresh readout template, trigger timing starts at the stored resonator flight time + 0.015 µs; verify the actual ADC window. Resolve required fields marked Invalid.
- **Shots** samples each cloud. Increase it if the fitted histogram is poorly sampled. **Relax delay** starts at 5 × stored ordinary T1 (150 µs if T1 is absent); invalid T1 leaves it blank. Reset does not shorten this wait automatically.
- **Initial State** names the predominant state after reset/init and before the probe. If it is `excited`, probe off maps to e and probe on to g. **Backend** rotates along the pooled PCA axis (`pca`) or the line between the centers (`center`).
- **Align T1** shares the decay ratio across histogram fits. **Length ratio (a.u.)** is readout duration / T1. Leave it blank to estimate it from the tail between the clouds. Fit shows the joint estimate ± its standard error, or `unavailable` when that error cannot be computed. The diagnostic shows separate g/e estimates when Align T1 is off. Fit shows a supplied ratio without a fit error. The numeric summary can report `length_ratio: null` without invalidating the centers, fidelity or Post result.
- **Log Scale** switches the y-axis of all three Diagnostic histograms from linear to logarithmic. It leaves the raw IQ plots and fitting unchanged.
- In the `singleshot_ge` recipe, `target` selects the qubit for the GE tab (for example, `Q1`). Set `reuse_tab_id` to reuse an idle matching GE tab. The recipe runs the tab's **Reset** to refresh its Form capture before setting experiment inputs. This tab Reset is not the optional shot Reset, which `use_reset` controls.
- In `singleshot_ge`, omitted experiment inputs keep the form or Primary settings; `use_reset: null` and `init_pulse_ref: null` disable those modules. Null `readout_ref` or `pi_ref` fails at required-module selection. Null `shots` clears the shot count; if no override fills it, the readiness check returns `needs_parameters` before Run. Null `initial_state`, `backend`, `logscale` or `align_t1` fails at Primary option validation, after Run; null `length_ratio` requests estimation. The recipe saves the completed Run even if analysis fails.

## How to read the results

- **Live** labels probe off in blue and probe on in red. The title lists the acquired probe branches and the cumulative shots across both branches. **Analyze Fit** maps the same I/Q shots (a.u.) to g preparation in blue and e preparation in red using **Initial State**. Each IQ bin mixes blue and red using the sixth powers of the two shot fractions, normalized to sum to one. A 60:40 split gives about 92:8 in color; equal counts give purple. Opacity rises with the log of the total shot count, and empty bins are transparent. These are shot distributions, not means with shot SEM bars.
- **Diagnostic** shows raw, equal-scale I/Q (a.u.) and the fitted centers at top left. The three histograms use `Projected I (a.u.)`, the I coordinate after rotation. The bottom left overlays both preparations with the fitted curves; `Fraction per bin` is normalized separately for each preparation. The text between the rows gives the prepared-label and ideal-model assignment scores. On the right, compare the fitted components and fractions for g preparation above and e preparation below. **Fitted SNR** is the projected center separation divided by the fitted common width; ± is the standard error from the same joint fit. `unavailable` marks an estimate the fit cannot report.
- In the Primary summary, `theta` is the angle (rad) used to rotate raw IQ before projecting onto I. `threshold` is the position of the dashed classification boundary on the projected I axis. `ge_s` is the shared Gaussian standard deviation of the fitted g and e peaks. Both `threshold` and `ge_s` use the same a.u. scale as the IQ coordinates.
- For the joint histogram fit, R² measures explained variation, normalized residual RMS measures mismatch relative to the histogram range, and the relative width and center errors compare fitted standard errors with the magnitudes of the fitted values. A poor fit makes inferred preparation mixtures and the Post correction unreliable, even if an assignment score is available.
- **Post-Analysis** classifies by nearest center inside its radius, not by the Primary threshold. Its left column shows raw I/Q for g preparation in blue above and e preparation in red below. The two panels share I and Q limits and keep equal I/Q scale. Opacity follows the log of the shot count in each bin; color does not indicate the classified label. Both panels mark the fitted centers and radius boundaries. The segment midway between the centers appears only inside the overlap of the two disks. When the circles are separate, there is no middle segment. Read the observed g/e/Other fractions above each panel before comparing the matrices: **Initial Populations** estimates the mixture for each preparation, and **Classifier confusion** estimates measured outcomes by state after removing that mixture. The condition number measures how sensitive the correction is to input errors. Other (`|L⟩`) is a rejected class, not an identified leakage level.

## Next step

Primary preselection checks joint-fit quality. If it is unchecked, read the **Writeback** note. Post must also have a finite condition number ≤ 10 for preselection. A candidate left unchecked is still selectable. Inspect IQ separation and fit quality before accepting either proposal; if the clouds overlap, check preparation and readout before rerunning.

Writeback replaces the Run's bound readout module classifier: Primary writes g/e centers, and Post adds radius and the 3×3 confusion matrix. Primary applied *after* Post clears the radius and matrix. Post can be accepted on its own; the recipe asks separately for each stage. Writeback requires the measured readout settings to match the captured library module apart from its classifier. Changing readout settings does not invalidate the stored classifier automatically; rerun GE and Post before using it for the new settings.
